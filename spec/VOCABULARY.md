# Simpli Vocabulary

- **Concept:** English-derived, simplified and phonetic.
- **Base:** ~300–500 essential words for everyday use but could easily be extended as needed.
- **Compounds:** New ideas from existing words (sun-light, langwij-study).

See the [Vocabulary](/en/vocabulary) and [Essential words](/en/essential-words) pages on the website for the full list by category.
