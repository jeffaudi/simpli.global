# Simpli Orthography

The sound of each letter is defined in [PRONUNCIATION.md](PRONUNCIATION.md). This file is the spelling rules. The archived v0.1 specification is a snapshot and can lag behind these living notes.

## Alphabet

- **Vowels:** a, e, i, o, u. A doubled vowel (aa, ee, ii, oo, uu) may be used for extra clarity. In general, do not.
- **Consonants:** p, b, t, d, k, g, m, n, f, s, l, r, h, **v**, w, y. **No letter c** (removed entirely). Use **k** for /k/. The sound /tʃ/ is written **tj**; /dʒ/ is written **dj** (symmetrical with tj). **G** always = /g/ (hard g). Write **w** for /w/ when English spells that sound with **w**, and **v** for /v/.

No *th*, *z*, *sh*; mapped to nearest letter (see below).

## Letter mappings

- **th** — voiceless /θ/ → t (*tingk*, *tri*, *bot*); voiced /ð/ → d (*dis*, *dat*, *dey*). This matches the word list. The archived v0.1 text still says every *th* becomes d.
- **v / w** — Use **w** when English spells the sound with **w** (at the start or inside the word): *woter* (water), *watever* (whatever), *wi*, *windo*. Use **v** when English uses **v**: *seven*, *river*, *hav*, *giv*, *liv*, *mov*, *even*. The reference pronunciations are /v/ and /w/. Saying them the same way is widely understood, but it merges pairs such as *veri*/*weri*, *wi*/*vi*, and *van*/*wan*.
- **ch** → **tj** (no letter c in Simpli): *tjaild* (child), *titjer* (teacher), *tjeindj* (change), *tjis* (cheese). For /k/ we always use **k**: *skul* (school), *akson* (action).
- **ph** → f (*fon*)
- **sh** → s; **z** → s when needed
- **g** = /g/ (always hard): *go*, *gud* (good), *giv* (give), *langwidj* (language). G never represents /dʒ/.
- **dj** = /dʒ/ (symmetrical with **tj** = /tʃ/): *djob* (job), *djeneral* (general), *djaiant* (giant), *meidjer* (major), *bridj* (bridge)
- **ou** — The sound /uː/ or /ʊ/ is written **u**. Standard spelling is *yu* (you); *you* is acceptable for clarity or familiarity.

## Rules

1. **One sound = one spelling, written with one or two letters;** no silent letters (*lait*, *windo*, *dor*, *bred*, *faind*, *plei*). Diphthongs and **tj** / **dj** use two letters.
2. **Long vowels are optional.** A doubled vowel (aa, ee, ii, oo, uu) may be used for extra clarity. In general, do not: one vowel letter is enough. The vowel of *father* and *hat* is **a**. When English writes that vowel with **o**, Simpli keeps **o** (*hot*, *not*, *on*, *stop*). Two words may then share a spelling: *not* and *note* are both *not*, *on* and *own* are both *on*. Diphthongs are one syllable: /ai/ → **ai** (*bai*, *tjaild*), /ei/ → **ei** (*plei*), /au/ → **ao** (*hao* how, *nao* now), /oi/ → **oi** (*boi*, *point*). Do not write **au** for /au/. Any other vowel pair is two syllables.
3. **Syllables and stress:** The usual shape is consonant + vowel + optional consonant. The word list also keeps clusters such as *str* and *nk*. Stress the second-to-last syllable (*kampyuter*, *yesterde*, *tumoro*). Do not reduce other vowels to a weak schwa. A short inserted vowel is understandable while learning; it is not the reference.
4. **Common endings:** -tion/-sion → -son (*akson*, *vison*), -ture → -tur, -ous → -us.
5. **ou → u:** Standard writing is **yu** (you); **you** is acceptable for clarity or acceptability.
6. **Double consonants:** Not encouraged, as they go against simplification. Prefer single consonant: *adres* (not *address*). Double consonants are acceptable but the simplified form is preferred. **g** after **ng** is kept (*fingger*, *ingglis*): that is ŋg, not a doubled letter.
